# Luminore: Sculptural Warmth

Approved direction: gender-inclusive, fashion-forward jewelry with parchment, olive, espresso and brass; Cormorant Garamond headings and Satoshi UI. User requested implementation after reviewing the section sequence and authorized existing product images and placeholders.

## Approach
Considered Editorial Glamour (high contrast), After-Dark Luxury (photography-heavy), and Sculptural Warmth (warm gallery aesthetic). User selected Sculptural Warmth. Extend the existing Shopify/Dawn theme with native editable sections, not a new framework.

## Experience
Announcement → sticky header → campaign hero → categories → Signature Edit → collection feature → Styled Your Way → details → brand story → newsletter → footer.
Hero and collection photography have restrained scroll parallax, reduced on mobile and disabled for reduced-motion preferences. Native scroll, visible content without JavaScript, keyboard-accessible navigation, responsive images and real product links/prices.

## Content and boundaries
Use existing catalog images as interim editorial imagery. Image pickers allow later campaign photography. Do not invent material, shipping, review, or craftsmanship claims. Global header/footer and typography coordinate with the new homepage; product purchasing remains in the existing Shopify product templates/cart. Preserve unrelated in-progress work.

## Design contract
Parchment #F1EDE3, olive #343A2E, espresso #29251F, brass #A58C63. Primary action: explore jewelry. Clear serif hierarchy, small uppercase labels, square photographic frames, generous but varied spacing. Product grids remain calm and readable. Mobile uses two product columns and stacked editorials. Avoid scroll hijacking, autoplay carousels, gratuitous gradients and decorative UI cards.
